Live TV Images (TMDB/TVDB)

Adds missing movie and series images to the TV guide.

Requires TheMovieDb. TheTVDB is also required when the series fallback is enabled. Providers are checked before each run.

Runs every five minutes and after guide refresh. Each batch searches new titles; cached matches are reused for all matching programmes. Unused matches expire after seven days; the cache is limited to 5,000 titles.

Run manually from Scheduled Tasks → Live TV → Update Live TV Images.
